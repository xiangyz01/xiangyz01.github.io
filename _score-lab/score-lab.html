About SCORE Lab

SCORE Lab (Systems Control, Optimization, Reliability & Engineering) is affiliated with the Intelligent Control and Intelligent Systems Team, School of Automation, Nanjing University of Science and Technology (NJUST). The group currently consists of 4 faculty members, 11 Ph.D. students, and several master's students.

Our research focuses on intelligent systems and control, with particular interests in control theory, control engineering, optimization, reliability, and autonomous systems. The group has undertaken multiple research projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) as well as provincial- and ministerial-level key research and development programs.

课题组简介

SCORE Lab（Systems Control, Optimization, Reliability & Engineering） 隶属于南京理工大学自动化学院智能控制与智能系统团队。课题组目前有 4 名教师、11 名博士研究生及若干硕士研究生。

课题组致力于智能系统与控制领域的研究工作，重点关注控制理论、控制工程、优化、可靠性与自主系统等方向。近年来承担了多项国家自然科学基金项目以及省部级重点研发项目。
